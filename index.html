Who We Are

Dedicated to Farmer Prosperity & Green Innovation

Muthu Farming is a premier agricultural equipment and farm infrastructure enterprise based in Coimbatore. For over 15 years, we have been committed to advancing modern agricultural techniques across South India.

From precision drip irrigation and solar water pumping systems to heavy-duty power tillers, rotavators, and polyhouse fabrication, we supply reliable solutions engineered specifically for Indian soil conditions.
