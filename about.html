Our Heritage

Pioneering Smart & Sustainable Agriculture

Founded in 2010 in dharmapuri, Muthu Farming has grown into a trusted agriculture technology leader serving farmers, plantation owners, and horticultural enterprises across South India.

Our core mission centers on maximizing crop productivity while conserving precious natural resources—providing precision drip irrigation systems, high-efficiency rotavators, solar pumping setups, and protective polyhouse structures.
